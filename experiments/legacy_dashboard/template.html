What is an EP dictionary?

Exemplar Partitioning tiles a model's residual-stream activation space into Voronoi cells, each anchored by a real observed activation — the exemplar. No training: activations are centered, unit-normalized, and leader-clustered with a cosine-distance threshold θ set from a percentile of pairwise distances.

Every dot = one region; every member = one token

Activations are harvested at every token position of every prompt (skipping position 0). A region's members are token activations, not whole prompts — the highlighted token in each excerpt is the exact token whose layer-L activation fell in the region, and the top excerpt at distance 0.000 is the exemplar itself.

Reading the numbers

d = cosine distance to the exemplar (members satisfy d ≤ θ). Coherence ∈ [0,1] = how tightly member directions agree. Logit lens = the exemplar direction pushed through the final norm + unembedding: the vocabulary this direction points toward. By construction no two exemplars are closer than θ.

J-lens and verbalizability

The logit lens unembeds a direction directly — it asks what the direction looks like in vocabulary space at its own layer, which can mislead at mid-stack. The Jacobian lens (Anthropic's jlens) first transports the direction to the final layer through J̄l, the corpus-averaged Jacobian ∂hfinal/∂hl, then unembeds — what the activation is disposed to make the model say. Per region we also store how concentrated the J-lens distribution is: verbalizability = 1 − H/ln|V| (entropy of the softmax at RMS-1 scale) plus the top-8 probability mass. High = a genuinely verbalizable concept region; low = a mechanical region whose token labels shouldn't be trusted, even if they look confident. Lenses are available for Qwen3.5-4B (instruct) and Qwen3.5-2B-Base; no public lens exists for Qwen3.5-4B-Base or the Gemma dictionary's mirror.

Trusting the pictures

The Atlas (t-SNE / PCA) and Sphere (PCA→3D) compress ~2000 dimensions where exemplars are nearly orthogonal — positions and gap sizes are navigation aids only. Exact geometry lives in the numbers: neighbor cosines, d values, and θ are computed in the full space.
